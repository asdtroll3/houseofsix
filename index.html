Our Story

About
House of Six

Founded in 2018 by six friends who shared a single conviction: that a great haircut is not a luxury — it's a statement. We built our studio in the heart of Brooklyn as an antidote to the conveyor-belt salon experience.

Every detail of the space was chosen deliberately — warm lighting, curated playlists, and a pace that never feels rushed. We want you to leave not just looking your best, but feeling genuinely looked after.

Our six stylists bring diverse training from New York, London, and Paris. Collectively, we cover every technique, texture, and hair type. What unites us is a commitment to craft, honesty, and a good conversation.
